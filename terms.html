Purpose and authorised use

Afrispore Backups supports backup and recovery of the Afrispore environmental monitoring installation. It is for authorised administrators who have permission to access the source system and destination Google account. It is not a public storage service.

Administrator responsibilities

  • Use only accounts and data you are authorised to manage.
  • Protect Google authorisation credentials, encrypted archives and recovery keys. Keep recovery keys available separately from the monitored Pi.
  • Review backup results, storage availability and overdue-upload notices, and perform periodic restore tests.
  • Keep a separate full-system recovery image and verify that application versions and database settings are compatible before restoring.

Operation and limitations

Cloud backup is currently under configuration and testing. Scheduling does not guarantee a successful upload. Power loss, network outages, account authorisation, storage limits, software faults or hardware failure may interrupt it.

A recovery restores data only as of the selected successful snapshot. Later readings and cycle changes may be unavailable. A checksum confirms a byte comparison; it does not establish successful system boot, sensor accuracy or a completed recovery test.

The backup utility does not operate heaters, cooling or other process equipment and is not a substitute for local supervision or independent safety controls. Recovery actions must be performed by an authorised operator.

Third-party services

Google Drive and GitHub Pages are independent services governed by their own terms. Afrispore Backups is not a Google or GitHub product or an endorsement by either provider.

Stopping use

An administrator may stop scheduled backups, revoke Google access and remove stored archives. Revoking access does not itself erase copies already held in Drive, on the Pi or on other media. See the privacy policy for deletion information.
